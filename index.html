FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Just One More
Little Question?

Because a great stay starts with peace of mind.

Can I actually book a stay? +

No. This is a demo website for a fictional brand. The form only creates a local booking preview and sends nothing.

Are the prices final and up to date? +

All prices are for demonstration purposes only. The estimate is not a binding offer.

How is the stay price calculated? +

We multiply the nightly rate by the number of nights. For stays of four nights or more, we subtract 10% from the accommodation cost, then add extras.

Can we bring a dog? +

For this demo, the pet fee is $11 per night. Actual pet policies would need to be confirmed with the operator.

Where are the cabins located? +

MEZI LESY is a fictional concept and has no real address or map location. Photos are illustrative.

Can rates change by season? +

This demo uses the same rates all year. Seasonal rates and an availability calendar could be added to a real website.
